Applying for access

Aegis Rise is invite-only, so membership starts with a short application rather than an open sign-up. Go to /signup and fill out the Apply for Access form.

The Apply for Access screen at /signup

What to fill in

PasswordAt least 8 characters.
Chapter Code/Namee.g. NY-01 — get this from the person who invited you or your chapter admin.
Professional Titlee.g. Software Engineer.

2

Chapter approval

Your application goes into a queue that only your chapter's admins can see, under Admin → Approvals. An admin reviews your name, title, company, and bio, then approves or denies the request.

Heads up — no email arrives yet. Even though the confirmation screen says you'll be notified, automated approval emails aren't turned on yet. Don't wait for an inbox alert — instead, simply try logging in again after a day or two, or follow up directly with whoever manages your chapter.

If you try to log in before you're approved, you'll see this message instead of getting in:

SituationMessage you'll see
Still pending approvalYour account is awaiting admin approval.
Application denied / inactiveYour account is not eligible for access.

Both are normal parts of the process, not errors on your end. Once an admin approves you, log in again and you're in.

3

Your first login

Head to /login, enter your email and password, and click Authenticate.

The Secure Login screen at /login

On success you'll see a "Welcome back" confirmation and land directly on your Chapter Feed — the home base for everything your chapter is posting and sharing.

Tip: Bookmark /login — visiting the site root while signed out sends you here automatically, and while signed in it takes you straight to your feed.
4

Setting up your profile

There are two related screens, reached from the Profile link in the sidebar:

My Profile — /profile

Edit your Full Name, Professional Title, Company, and Biography — the information other members see on your posts. This page also shows your chapter, post count, share count, and the Social sharing connections (see Section 5).

Profile Settings — /profile/settings

This is where you customize how your profile looks, with a live preview as you edit.

What you'll see on Profile Settings
  • Update Photo and Upload Wallpaper buttons above a Live Profile Preview card, with a Wallpaper Size slider (50–200%) to resize your banner image inside the frame.
  • An Appearance & Customization card with an Interface Theme toggle (Dark Mode / Light Mode).
  • Identity Color — your main accent color — with six presets, or pick any custom color/hex:
Electric Blue Neon Purple Cyber Green Sunset Orange Crimson Red Goldenrod
  • Accent Color — secondary glow and highlights — same six presets or custom.
  • Profile Background — the base color behind your hero banner:
Deep Space Midnight Blue Dark Purple Crimson Void Forest Shadow
  • Click Save Changes — the button briefly confirms Saved! and a "Profile updated successfully" toast appears.

Example: a member who picked Neon Purple + Cyber Green + Dark Purple sees their initial-avatar ring, name, and title glow in that palette everywhere their profile is shown.

Tip: Set your profile photo, title/company, and colors before you connect social accounts or post — your chosen look carries through to your posts, shares, and public profile immediately.
5

Connecting social accounts

On My Profile, the Social sharing card lets you connect LinkedIn, Facebook, and Instagram so you can publish chapter posts straight to your own network.

What you'll see
  • Each platform row shows a Not connected badge and a Connect button. Clicking it opens that provider's login/consent screen in your browser — approve access there, and you're returned to your profile.
  • Once connected, a row shows either Publishing ready (green — you're good to go) or Reconnect needed (something needs fixing), plus a Disconnect button.
  • An Auto-post selected shares toggle and a Selected platforms checklist let you choose which connected accounts get used automatically when you share a post (Section 6).

Prerequisites & troubleshooting

PlatformWhat's requiredIf it's not eligible yet
LinkedIn A personal LinkedIn account. No special setup needed. Rare — usually resolves by reconnecting.
Facebook You must manage a Facebook Page (a personal-only profile isn't enough). Connect a Facebook Page you can publish to, then reconnect.
Instagram An Instagram Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page. Connect an Instagram Business or Creator account to a Facebook Page, then reconnect.

If you see "Reconnect needed" after connecting, it almost always means the prerequisite above isn't in place yet — fix it on the provider's side (e.g. convert to a Business account, or get Page access), then click Connect again.

If a connection attempt fails outright, you'll see a toast saying "Social connection was not completed" — this usually means the sign-in was cancelled or the provider account isn't ready. Just click Connect again once it is.
6

Creating & sharing posts

Your Chapter Feed (/feed) has a composer permanently at the top — there's no separate "new post" screen.

What you'll see — the composer
  • A text box with the placeholder "Share something with your chapter..." — a caption is required before you can post.
  • Add images — attach up to 10 JPEG/PNG/GIF images (shown as a live n/10 counter). Drag to reorder them, or use the arrow keys; hover to remove one.
  • A Post button that uploads your images first, then publishes — you'll get a "Post created successfully" toast and it appears instantly at the top of the feed.

To share an existing post — yours or a fellow member's — click Share on that post.

What you'll see — the share dialog
  • Share this post dialog with buttons for LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and Copy Link.
  • A preview box shows platform-ready share text generated for you.
  • Auto-post connected accounts — flip this on to publish immediately to the platforms you selected in Section 5, instead of only copying a link.
  • After sharing, a toast reports the outcome — "Post shared" if everything went through, or "Shared with some social posting issues" if a platform failed — with a per-platform breakdown (published, or the specific provider error) underneath.
Tip: If a platform reports an error when sharing, it's almost always the same prerequisite issue from Section 5 (e.g. Facebook needs a Page). Fix the connection once and every future share works.
7

Tips & best practices

  • Finish your profile before your first post. A photo, title, company, and a short bio make your posts feel credible in the feed and on shared links.
  • Pick colors that read well in dark mode. Lighter presets (Cyber Green, Goldenrod) pop more than darker ones (Crimson Red) against the default black background.
  • Connect socials once, then forget about it. After LinkedIn/Facebook/Instagram show "Publishing ready," sharing becomes a single click via auto-post.
  • Write a caption every time. The feed won't let you post without one — think of it as your headline.
  • Reorder images before posting, not after. Once a post is live, image order isn't editable — drag them into place in the composer first.
  • Didn't get approved yet? There's no email notification — check in with your chapter admin directly rather than waiting on your inbox.
